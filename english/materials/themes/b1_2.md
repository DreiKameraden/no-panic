## 6. READING 2 — The real world: Modern Work Trends (7 min)

The way we work is changing rapidly. In recent years, several major trends have emerged in the global job market.

First, the demand for **remote work** has skyrocketed. Many employees now refuse to return to the office full-time, valuing the flexibility to work from home. This has led to the rise of the **gig economy**, where people take on short-term contracts or freelance work instead of traditional 9-to-5 jobs.

Second, the concept of a **four-day work week** is being tested in countries like the UK and Iceland. Trials have shown that reducing working hours without cutting pay actually *increases* productivity and significantly reduces employee **burnout**.

However, challenges remain. The pressure **to be constantly connected** via smartphones and emails blurs the line between professional and personal life. Furthermore, with the rise of AI, many workers worry about **losing their jobs** to automation, forcing them to constantly **upskill** and adapt to new technologies.

## 7. GRAMMAR: Gerund vs. Infinitive (10 min)

В английском языке после некоторых глаголов мы можем использовать только **Gerund** (`-ing`), а после других — только **Infinitive** (`to + V1`). Это нужно для выражения предпочтений, планов и отношения к процессам.

### 📌 Основное правило

| Категория                                                      | Формула                   | Примеры глаголов                                         | Пример из текста / жизни                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gerund (`V-ing`)**                                            | Глагол + **-ing**    | *enjoy, avoid, consider, finish, mind, suggest, imagine, can't stand* | I enjoy **working** remotely.Kai avoids **thinking** about the System.                                                                              |
| **Infinitive (`to + V`)**                                       | Глагол + **to + V1** | *want, hope, plan, decide, promise, refuse, afford, expect*           | I want **to understand** this paradox.She plans **to apply** for a new job.                                                                         |
| **Оба варианта (с разницей в смысле)** | Зависит от смысла | *stop, remember, forget, try*                                         | **Stop working** (прекратить действие).**Stop to work** (остановиться, *чтобы* начать работать). |

### ⚠️ Важные нюансы (B2 Level)

1. **После предлогов** всегда используется **Gerund**:*He is good **at negotiating** salaries.She is worried **about losing** her job.*
2. **После выражений** *look forward to, be used to, object to* тоже идет **Gerund** (здесь `to` — это предлог!):*I look forward **to hearing** from you.Kai is not used **to having** free time.*
3. **Глаголы восприятия** (*see, hear, watch*) + объект + **V-ing** (процесс) или **V1** (факт):
   *I saw him **typing** the code.* (Я видел процесс).

## 6. READING 2 — The real world: how education works today

*Adapted B1–B2 text based on publicly documented facts about these systems. Original sources below.*

There is no single "modern education system" — there are dozens, and they are built on different beliefs.

In **Finland**, children start school at seven, homework is light, and national tests are almost absent until the final matriculation exam. Lessons are often organised around real-world "phenomena" rather than separate subjects, and teachers are trusted professionals with a master's degree.

In **Germany**, many teenagers choose a *dual* path: part of the week is spent at a company, part at a vocational school. Practical skills are not seen as a second-class option.

In **England**, the route is more exam-driven: GCSEs at sixteen, A-levels at eighteen, and university places are usually conditional on those grades. The **International Baccalaureate**, taken in over 150 countries, adds Theory of Knowledge, an extended essay and compulsory community work.

**Estonia** has moved much of its school administration and testing online, while the **OECD** compares all of these systems every few years through **PISA**, which tests 15-year-olds in reading, maths and science.

The debate everywhere is the same: efficiency versus wellbeing, standardisation versus individual needs — and, increasingly, what should be taught by a human and what by an algorithm.

## 7. GRAMMAR — Passive Voice (Present & Past Simple)

- Прочитайте грамматический урок **Passive Voice**: 👉 👉 [Пассивный залог](/no-panic/lesson.html?file=english/materials/grammar/passive.md&section=english)

### Why we need it

To describe **systems, rules and processes** when the doer is unknown, obvious or unimportant. Perfect for comparing "how things are done" in 2026 and 2126.

### Formula: `be + V3 (past participle)`

|                        | Active                   | Passive                                            |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Present**      | AI assigns mentors.      | Mentors **are assigned** (by AI).            |
| **Present neg.** | They don't write exams.  | Exams **aren't written**.                    |
| **Present ?**    | Do they test students?   | **Are** students **tested**?           |
| **Past**         | They banned paper books. | Paper books **were banned**.                 |
| **Past neg.**    | Nobody explained it.     | It **wasn't explained**.                     |
| **Past ?**       | Who wrote the report?    | Who **was** the report **written by**? |
